Solution


Introduction

With the main criteria for a minimum viable product being the location and time of the market, a mobile app would be best for the solution. A mobile app would streamline the user's experience by having the app downloaded on their phone. This would ensure easier and faster access to information. From the user's side, the mobile app would show the information of when the farmer’s market is open, as well as the location. A map will show the location of each particular stand, and users can see the fruits and vegetables that each stand offers.


Details

The current problem that farmers markets face is lack of rentention. There has been a demographic shift in consumers that regularly go to farmers markets, and has become a much more social experience, focusing more on food and drinks rather than fresh, organic produce (Carman, “For some growers, farmers markets just aren’t what they used to be”). However, it has been harder to appeal to this more technologically advanced demographic.

To combat this, a mobile app would be the best response to this demographic shift, and would appeal more strongly to its technological users. By more directly targetting this audience, consumer engagement and retention would increase, as would the sales of farmers markets produce. An user friendly app would help with promotion as well as increase in interests, therefore giving the app a higher success rate (Gauchet, “The Ultimate iPhone App Marketing Strategy Guide”).

Reception

Based off of the market research done on farmers markets, app market research, and an user research survey we conducted, we are anticipating a positive reaction to the app. From the responses in the survey, 92.7% said a mobile app would encourage users to go to farmers market more (Farmers Market Team, “Farmers Market Survey”). As the demographic shift in farmers market is leaning more towards a more technological audience, farmers markets must adapt in order to continue to appeal to its consumers. A mobile app would satisfy the consumer need, and not only keep them interested in farmers market, but encourage them to regularly attend.
