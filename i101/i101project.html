Scenario

Farmers of a local county are creating and utilizing market stands as a way of reaching the public and selling produce. It has become increasingly difficult to let customers know where and when they will be set up and be open for business.


More information and process

Process

  • Market research/trends, subject research, and user data used to find the best solution to the scenario

Scope

Criteria/Requirements
  • Users need to know the location of the farmer’s market
  • Users need to know the times the farmer’s market is open
  • Fruits/vegetables that each stand carries (list of available items)
User Base
  • Adults and older (able to make purchases)
